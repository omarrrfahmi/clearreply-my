What exactly am I buying?

A set of written materials for your cleaning business: one service description, eight FAQs and ten customer reply templates, plus one revision round. You receive editable English copy to use in your own customer conversations.

Is this right for my business?

This offer is designed for Malaysian residential cleaning businesses. It is most useful if you handle enquiries yourself and want consistent answers ready to personalise. Submit a consultation request and we will confirm whether it fits.

What do you need from me?

Your service areas, included and excluded tasks, pricing approach and booking policies. We clarify missing details with you rather than inventing them. Please remove customer personal information from any example messages.

Does applying commit me to anything?

No. A consultation request is not a purchase or a confirmed booking. We contact you first, confirm the scope and availability, and agree payment arrangements before work begins.
